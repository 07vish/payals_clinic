Common Questions

Everything you need to know.

Is root canal treatment really painless at your clinic?

Yes. Dr. Payal Chaudhari uses gentle local numbing techniques before any treatment begins, ensuring the tooth and surrounding tissue are fully numb. As verified by numerous patients in our reviews, procedures are gentle, calm, and pain-free.

Do I need an appointment beforehand or do you accept walk-ins?

We welcome both scheduled appointments and walk-in patients during our morning (10:30 AM–1:00 PM) and evening (5:00 PM–8:00 PM) hours. To ensure zero waiting time, we recommend reserving your time in advance.

Where is the clinic located and is parking available?

The clinic is situated on the 1st Floor of Mazda Mension, directly near Anand Hospital in Nanpura, Surat (395001). Street and dedicated commercial vehicle parking is available nearby.

How are instruments sterilized?

We adhere to hospital-grade, multi-stage sterilization. Instruments undergo ultrasonic pre-cleaning followed by high-pressure, vacuum Class-B autoclaving sealed in individual sterile pouches.
